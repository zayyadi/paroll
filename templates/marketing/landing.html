Reform-ready

Already on the 2026 tax reform.

The Nigeria Tax Act 2025 rewrote PAYE from 1 January 2026: new 0%–25% bands, a ₦800,000 tax-free threshold, rent relief replacing the old consolidated allowance, and fully exempt minimum-wage earners. While global vendors scrambled to update, PayNest ships the new regime as the default — with the pre-reform PITA bands kept as data for retrospective runs.

Governance as a feature

Statutory remittance is where trust breaks. PayNest makes it the product.

Set-and-forget payroll has a cost: when tax and pension remittances quietly fail, the employer — not the software — answers to the state. PayNest treats segregation of duties, audit trails, and remittance oversight as first-class payroll controls.

Segregation of duties

Payroll processors run operations; finance and HR see what their role requires — view-only where it should be — so no single actor controls calculation, approval, and remittance.

Audit trails

Sensitive access to payslips, bank details, and pension data is logged with who, when, and why — a reviewable record for internal and statutory scrutiny.

Remittance oversight

Every obligation is tracked to its due date, with overdue remittances flagged and pension penalty exposure (2% per month) surfaced before it compounds.
