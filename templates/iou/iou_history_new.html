IOU Policy Information

Important: IOU requests are salary advances that will be deducted from your future paychecks. Please review the terms carefully before submitting a request.

Maximum Amount

Advance limits are set by your company's policy - capped against earned-but-unpaid pay, with a take-home floor protecting your next salary.

Repayment Terms

Earned Wage Access advances are repaid automatically from your next pay cycle through payroll deduction.

Approval Process

All advance requests require manager approval and are subject to company policy.

Repayment Deduction

The repayment deduction percentage is set by the company when approving your request.
