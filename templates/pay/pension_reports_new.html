Pension Contribution Information

Important: Pension contributions must be remitted to the pension fund administrator within 7 working days of salary payment (PenCom); late remittance attracts 2% per month of the outstanding amount. The 7-working-day clock runs from the run's stored salary payment date; where that hasn't been recorded it falls back to the period start.

Report Format

The pension reports are generated in Excel format compatible with pension fund administration systems.

Contribution Requirements

Pension contribution reports are due with the remittance within the 7-working-day window — track each run's deadline and mark it remitted in the compliance calendar.
