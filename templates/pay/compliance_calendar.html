Rule basis

  • PAYE monthly remittance: due by the 10th of the month following salary payment (Nigeria Tax Act 2025 / state IRS practice).
  • PAYE annual emoluments return: due 31 January of the following year.
  • Pension: remit within 7 working days of salary payment (PenCom); late remittance attracts 2% per month of the outstanding amount. The 7-working-day clock runs from the run's stored salary payment date; where that hasn't been recorded it falls back to the period start. Working days skip weekends and company public holidays.
  • NHF and NHIA: no fixed remittance date in the primary acts — the 10th of the following month is the practice-based default; penalties are per the NHF Act/FMBN and the NHIA Act/plan terms respectively.
  • NSITF (Employees' Compensation Act 2010): 1% of monthly payroll, remitted on or before the last day of the month (s.13(2) practice); shown when the company's NSITF flag is on and a levy amount exists.
  • ITF training levy: 1% of annual payroll, remitted within one month of the close of the financial year (practice cites 1 April); shown per year when the company's ITF flag is on. Amount is computed from current payroll configs.
